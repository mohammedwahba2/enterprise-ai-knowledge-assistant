# Data Privacy and Retention Policy (POL-DP-021)

Falcon Logistics (synthetic company used only for testing). This policy governs personal data of customers, drivers and employees.

## 1. Scope

The policy applies to every system, spreadsheet and paper file that holds personal data processed by Falcon Logistics, including data held by suppliers on our behalf.

## 2. Retention periods

Customer delivery records are kept for 7 years. Driver telematics data is kept for 13 months. CCTV footage from depots is kept for 30 days unless an incident is under investigation. CVs of unsuccessful job applicants are deleted 12 months after the hiring decision.

## 3. Requests from individuals

A request to access, correct or delete personal data must be answered within 30 calendar days. Every request is recorded in the privacy register by the Data Protection Officer, who also decides whether an exemption applies.

## 4. Breach notification

A suspected personal data breach must be reported to the Data Protection Officer within 24 hours of discovery. If the breach is likely to harm individuals, the regulator is notified within 72 hours and the affected individuals are informed without undue delay.

## 5. Sharing with processors

Personal data may be shared with a processor only under a signed data processing agreement. Each processor is reviewed every 12 months, and the review covers security controls, sub-processors and deletion on exit.

## 6. Transfers outside the Kingdom

Personal data may leave the Kingdom only to countries on the approved list maintained by the Legal department, and only after a transfer impact assessment has been signed by the Data Protection Officer.

## 7. Training

Staff who handle personal data complete privacy training every year. New joiners complete it within 30 days of their start date, and access to the customer database is withheld until they have.
