# Information Security Standard

Falcon Logistics (synthetic company used only for testing). This standard applies to every employee and contractor.

## 1. Passwords and authentication

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all remote access and for administrator accounts.

## 2. Reporting incidents

Any security incident must be reported to the IT service desk within 24 hours of discovery. Suspected phishing emails must be reported within 1 hour using the report-phishing button.

## 3. Laptops and devices

All company laptops use full-disk encryption. USB storage devices are blocked by default and may be enabled only with written approval from the security team.

## 4. Training

Every employee completes security awareness training once per year, and a phishing simulation is run every quarter.

## 5. Data classification

Documents are classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Restricted documents may only be shared with named individuals and must never be sent to personal email accounts.

## 6. Software installation

Employees may not install software on their own. Requests are made through the service desk and are answered within 2 working days.
