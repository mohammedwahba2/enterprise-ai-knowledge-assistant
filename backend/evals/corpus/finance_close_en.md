# Month-End Close Calendar (POL-FN-019)

Falcon Logistics (synthetic company used only for testing). This calendar applies to every legal entity in the group.

## 1. Timeline

The books for each month are closed on working day 5. Expense accruals are submitted by working day 2, bank reconciliations are completed by working day 3 and intercompany balances are matched by working day 4.

## 2. Accrual threshold

Expenses are accrued when the amount exceeds SAR 2,000 and the goods or services were received before month end, even if no invoice has arrived.

## 3. Journal entries

Manual journal entries above SAR 100,000 require approval from the Chief Financial Officer. Every manual entry needs a written explanation and supporting documents attached in the ledger system.

## 4. Fixed assets

Items are capitalised as fixed assets when the cost exceeds SAR 10,000 and the useful life is more than one year. Depreciation starts in the month the asset is placed in service.

## 5. Budget variances

Any variance against budget of more than 10 percent, in either direction, must be explained by the cost centre owner in the monthly pack.

## 6. Reporting

The management accounts are published to the executive team on working day 7. A late close must be reported to the Chief Financial Officer by working day 5.

## 7. Records

The audit trail of the ledger, including approvals and reversals, is kept for 10 years.
