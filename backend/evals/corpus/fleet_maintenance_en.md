# Fleet Maintenance and Driver Safety

Falcon Logistics (synthetic company used only for testing). This procedure covers all company trucks and vans.

## 1. Service intervals

Every vehicle is serviced every 10,000 km or every 6 months, whichever comes first. Service records are kept in the fleet system for the life of the vehicle.

## 2. Tyre checks

Tyre pressure and tread depth are checked weekly by the driver and recorded in the vehicle log. Tyres with less than 3 mm of tread are replaced immediately.

## 3. Driving hours

Drivers may drive a maximum of 9 hours per day. A mandatory break of 45 minutes is required after 4.5 hours of continuous driving.

## 4. Accidents

Every accident must be reported to the fleet control room within 1 hour, and a police report must be obtained before the vehicle is moved, unless it blocks traffic.

## 5. Speed monitoring

The telematics system raises an alert when a vehicle exceeds 110 km/h. Three alerts in one month lead to a mandatory driver coaching session.

## 6. Fuel cards

Fuel cards are protected by a personal PIN and may only be used for the vehicle they are assigned to.
