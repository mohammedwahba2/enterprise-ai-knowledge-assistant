# New Joiner Onboarding and Probation Guide (POL-HR-014)

Falcon Logistics (synthetic company used only for testing). This guide covers the first three months of employment.

## 1. Probation period

Every new employee serves a probation period of 90 days. During probation either side may end the employment with 7 days written notice. After probation the notice period is 60 days for staff and 90 days for managers.

## 2. First day

On the first day the employee receives an ID badge, building access and a laptop. IT must deliver the laptop and accounts within 1 working day of the start date; the line manager confirms delivery in the HR portal.

## 3. Buddy programme

A colleague from the same department is assigned as a buddy for the first 30 days. The buddy answers day-to-day questions and introduces the new joiner to at least five people in other teams.

## 4. Reviews

Formal review meetings take place at day 30, day 60 and day 90. The day 90 review decides whether the employee is confirmed, the probation is extended once by up to 30 days, or the employment ends.

## 5. Mandatory induction

The safety induction must be completed within the first week, and the information security and data privacy courses within the first 30 days. Employees who miss a deadline are reported to their line manager.

## 6. Relocation support

Employees who relocate to take the job receive a one-time relocation allowance of SAR 5,000, paid with the first salary after receipts are submitted.

## 7. Confirmation letter

HR issues the confirmation letter within 10 working days after a successful day 90 review. The letter states the confirmed grade, salary and leave entitlement.
