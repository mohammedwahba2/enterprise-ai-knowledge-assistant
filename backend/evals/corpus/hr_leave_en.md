# Leave Policy

Falcon Logistics (synthetic company used only for testing). This policy applies to all full-time employees.

## 1. Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 25 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly. Employees with more than five years of service receive 30 working days of paid annual leave.

## 2. Carry-over of unused leave

Unused annual leave may be carried over for a maximum of 5 days. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March of the following year; any carried-over days not used by that date lapse without compensation.

## 3. Sick leave

Employees are entitled to 15 days of sick leave at full pay per year, followed by 15 days at half pay. A medical certificate is required for any sick absence longer than 2 consecutive days.

## 4. Parental leave

Mothers receive 70 days of paid maternity leave. Fathers receive 3 days of paid paternity leave, to be taken within the first month after the birth of the child.

## 5. Requesting leave

Requests are submitted through the HR portal at least 14 days before the start date for any leave longer than 5 days. The line manager must approve or reject the request within 3 working days, otherwise it is escalated to the department head.

## 6. Public holidays

Employees who are required to work on an official public holiday receive another day off in lieu, to be taken within 60 days.
