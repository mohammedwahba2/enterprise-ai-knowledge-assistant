# Customer Complaints Handling Procedure (POL-CS-032)

Falcon Logistics (synthetic company used only for testing). This procedure applies to every complaint received by any channel.

## 1. Acknowledgement

Every complaint is acknowledged within 4 business hours, in the language the customer used, Arabic or English, and receives a reference number.

## 2. Categories

Complaints are classified as delay, damage, loss or billing. The category decides which team owns the case and which evidence is required.

## 3. Resolution targets

Standard complaints must be resolved within 5 working days. If a case is still open after 2 working days the owner escalates it to the team supervisor, who reviews it daily until it is closed.

## 4. Damage claims

A claim for damaged goods requires photographs of the goods and the packaging, submitted within 48 hours of delivery. Claims without photographs are reviewed only at the discretion of the customer service manager.

## 5. Compensation limits

A team lead may approve compensation of up to SAR 500 for a late delivery. Amounts above SAR 500 require approval from the customer service manager, and amounts above SAR 5,000 from the operations director.

## 6. Callbacks

When a customer asks for a callback it must happen within 1 business day, and the agent must record the outcome of the call in the case notes.

## 7. Satisfaction

A satisfaction survey is sent after every closed complaint. The monthly target is an average of at least 4.5 out of 5, and the result is reviewed by the customer service manager.
