# Travel and Expense Policy

Falcon Logistics (synthetic company used only for testing). All business travel must be approved before booking.

## 1. Daily allowance

The daily allowance (per diem) is SAR 300 per day for domestic travel and SAR 450 per day for international travel. The allowance covers meals and local transport, but not accommodation.

## 2. Flights

Employees fly economy class on flights shorter than 6 hours. Business class is allowed only on flights longer than 6 hours and requires written approval from a director.

## 3. Hotels

The maximum hotel rate is SAR 800 per night for domestic trips and SAR 1,200 per night for international trips. Bookings above the cap need approval from the finance manager.

## 4. Receipts

An original receipt or electronic invoice is required for every expense above SAR 75. Expenses without a receipt are reimbursed only with a signed declaration and only once per quarter.

## 5. Expense reports

Expense reports must be submitted within 10 working days after the end of the trip. Reports submitted later than 30 days are not reimbursed.

## 6. Travel advances

Employees may request a travel advance of up to 50 percent of the estimated trip cost, submitted at least 5 days before departure.
