# Warehouse Safety Procedure (POL-WH-007)

Falcon Logistics (synthetic company used only for testing). This procedure applies to all depots and cross-dock facilities.

## 1. Forklift operation

Only employees holding a valid forklift licence may operate a forklift. The speed limit inside the warehouse is 8 km/h, and horns must be sounded at every blind corner. Mobile phones must not be used while operating a forklift.

## 2. Protective equipment

Everyone on the warehouse floor wears a hard hat, a high-visibility vest and safety shoes. Visitors are escorted at all times and receive the same equipment at the gate.

## 3. Stacking

Pallets may be stacked a maximum of 4 high, and only on racking that has passed its annual inspection. Damaged pallets are removed from circulation and tagged red.

## 4. Fire safety

Fire drills are held every 6 months, and the evacuation time target is 4 minutes. Extinguishers are checked monthly and aisles must keep a clear width of 1.2 metres.

## 5. First aid

First-aid kits are inspected every month by the safety officer. There must be one trained first aider for every 25 employees on a shift.

## 6. Loading bays

Wheel chocks are placed under the wheels of every trailer before loading starts. The bay door is not opened until the driver has handed the keys to the dock supervisor.

## 7. Incident reporting

Any injury or near miss must be reported to the shift supervisor within 2 hours and entered in the safety log by the end of the shift. Serious injuries are reported to the safety manager immediately.

## 8. Lighting

Night-shift areas must be lit to at least 200 lux, and lights are tested every week.
